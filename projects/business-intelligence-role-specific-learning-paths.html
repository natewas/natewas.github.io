Business Intelligence Role-Specific Learning Paths

Created 15 microlearning courses in Rise 360 to help customers and internal employees transition to a new BI tool, simplifying complex data concepts for both analysts and low-access end users. Content, SME reviews, and publication all shipped inside a three-week sprint.

Microlearning

15 courses to launch a new BI tool.

A structured path from basic to advanced concepts, built in Rise 360.

Built for Everyone

Impact

Fifteen courses in twenty days.

Accelerated adoption with minimal disruption, replacing costly 1:1 training.

Challenge

The company transitioned from a legacy business-analytics platform to a new one. Every customer needed training on how to use the new tool to generate reports and monitor essential metrics, but existing training resources became outdated practically overnight.

Solution

I developed a structured, scalable learning experience in Rise 360, starting with 10 courses and expanding to 15 based on user needs. Courses built on each other, introducing basic to advanced concepts.

  • Included multimedia, interactive modules, and built-in assessments.
  • Targeted both analysts and low-access users with customized examples.
  • Reviewed by SMEs and published within a tight 20-day sprint.

My Role

  • Designed the full course-track structure and learning objectives.
  • Wrote scripts, built courses, and produced interactive content in Rise 360.
  • Collaborated with SMEs and QA reviewers to validate accuracy and clarity.

Tools & Technologies

  • Authoring: Rise 360, Adobe XD
  • Development: HTML, CSS (for supplemental web content)
  • Collaboration: Google Docs, Slack, Zoom
