Challenge

The Certified Admin exams included over 80 unique short-answer questions across eight assessments, each requiring manual grading. The process was time-consuming, inconsistent across graders, and difficult to scale.

Delivering contextual, personalized feedback was also hard given vague, overly detailed, or partially correct responses from learners of varying backgrounds.

Solution

An engine trained on course materials, answer keys, and company context handles vague, nuanced, and over-detailed answers, while excluding biased or incorrect historical data.

Generates precise, supportive feedback (e.g. "missing concept," "good structure, incomplete logic") without pass/fail bias.

Rubric-based scoring, aligned with SME validation and refined through blind comparison against human graders.

My Role

  • Led project design and development from ideation to pilot testing.
  • Authored the custom prompt architecture and validation scripts.
  • Ran blind testing with SMEs and iterated based on feedback.
  • Designed the rollout and data-governance framework.
