Summary: I designed and built an automated localization system to make 750 courses available in 15 languages. The solution integrates a custom pre- and post-processing utility that parses SCORM packages, leverages SmartCat’s command-line interface (CLI) for translation, and reinserts the content, enabling full course localization (text, UI, captions, and media) within 24 hours. The result: drastically reduced manual effort, improved compliance, and a scalable foundation for global training success.
